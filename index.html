04 · Interactions

The little touches, in plain words

Just vanilla JavaScript plus a tiny GSAP cameo for the hero entrance. No heavy libraries. (Hover these cards — this is the real effect.)

3D card tilt

Cards lean toward your mouse as you move across them. Subtle, not loud.

Cursor glow

Each card has a soft cyan light inside that follows your cursor.

Cursor halo

A faint cyan dot trails your mouse everywhere on the page.

Smooth fade-ins

Sections gently fade up as you scroll down. No sudden jumps.

Counting numbers

The stat numbers count from zero to their target when you scroll past.

Active pill

Clicked category pills fill with cyan to show what's selected.

05 · Accessibility

a11y means accessibility.

The 11 stands for the eleven letters between 'a' and 'y'.

Blind users
Navigate using screen readers
Keyboard only
Tab key, arrow keys, no mouse
Low vision
Need high contrast and big text
Color blind
Cannot rely on color alone

Our homepage works for all of them.

06 · Standards

WCAG 2.1 AA

Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, version 2.1, level AA.

What it is

The global rulebook for web accessibility. Three levels: A (minimum), AA (industry standard), AAA (strictest). AA is what BYU, government sites, and most companies require.

What we pass
  • Text contrast 4.5 to 1
  • Keyboard navigation works
  • Focus rings visible
  • Reduced motion respected

The homepage passes.
